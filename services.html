Service one

A conversation that tells you whether this is worth doing

Before anything is priced, Lance wants to understand the job. You describe what you’re trying to achieve; he asks the questions that change the answer — condition, access, timing, what’s already been attempted — and tells you honestly if it isn’t something he should take on.

It’s the fastest part of the process and the part that saves the most money. Guessing is what makes jobs expensive.

Service two

A written quote with the scope spelled out

Once Lance has what he needs, you get a quote that says what is included, what is excluded, and what would change the number. No bundled “miscellaneous” line, no vague allowances you only understand after the invoice arrives.

If a line item doesn’t make sense, call and ask about it. You’ll be put through to the person who wrote the quote, because that’s the only person answering the phone.
